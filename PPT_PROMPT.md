# One-shot AI presentation prompt

Create a complete 12-slide, 16:9 startup pitch deck for **CampusPulse**, a Smart Campus Challenge project for a college audience and hackathon judges. Output a polished editable presentation, not a plan. Do not include speaker notes, citations, placeholders, or generic stock photos.

## Design system
- Dark charcoal background: `#202137`; primary accent mint: `#BFF6D8`; supporting violet: `#786CF0`; warm coral: `#FFAB9A`.
- Use Manrope or a comparable bold geometric sans. Titles 50pt+, body 18pt+. Use a small monospace eyebrow for slide number and section.
- Style: premium, calm, editorial startup—not a busy dashboard. Large type, generous whitespace, subtle tinted cards only where they clarify information.
- Include a tiny `CampusPulse` wordmark footer on every slide. Use clean line icons, abstract orbs, and simple geometric motifs; no external logos.
- Keep every slide under 35 visible words where possible. Max 6 bullets, max 8 words each. Never overflow or wrap a headline onto an awkward extra line.
